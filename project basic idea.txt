# HeatSafe India — Complete Project Brief (Pune Edition)

Hyperlocal Heat-Health Risk Intelligence Platform
A web app that estimates which neighbourhoods in Pune face greater heat-health risk, explains why, and helps decision-makers prioritize preventive action.

## 1. Problem Statement
Traditional weather forecasts give a single citywide temperature for Pune (e.g. Shivajinagar weather station), but heat-health risk varies sharply across wards:
- Humidity affects evaporative cooling and wet-bulb risk.
- High surface heat from asphalt and dense concrete roofs amplifies urban heat island (UHI) effects.
- Tree canopy and shade vary drastically (e.g., Koregaon Park vs. Old Peths).
- Socio-economic vulnerability (slums, outdoor informal workers, dense senior populations) determines resilience.
- Air pollution (particulate matter / ozone in industrial/traffic corridors) compounds thermal distress.

Goal: Transition from uniform city alerts to ward-level, explainable, actionable heat-health intelligence for Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC) and public health planners.

## 2. Core Functional Requirements
1. **Interactive Ward Heat-Risk Map (Pune)**:
   - Centered on Pune, Maharashtra (18.5204° N, 73.8567° E).
   - Six distinct ward zones with polygon boundaries & risk color coding (Low, Moderate, High, Very High).
   - Tooltips, click-to-inspect, and visual risk indicators.

2. **Hyperlocal Risk Engine**:
   - Formula: R = 0.30*T + 0.20*H + 0.20*S + 0.20*V + 0.10*A
   - T: Temperature-related heat score (30%)
   - H: Humidity-related heat stress / heat index (20%)
   - S: Surface heat / impervious surface factor (20%)
   - V: Socio-economic & demographic vulnerability (20%)
   - A: Air quality index / particulate burden (10%)
   - Tiers: Low (0-24, Green), Moderate (25-49, Yellow), High (50-74, Orange), Very High (75-100, Red).

3. **Explainable Ward Intelligence Panel**:
   - Clean Civic Light Theme UI (municipal dashboard aesthetic).
   - Ward score badge, risk category, and plain-language driver breakdown (e.g., "Driven by 88% concrete density and low canopy in historical core").
   - Comparative factor breakdown (radar / bar chart).

4. **Targeted Prevention Playbooks**:
   - Context-sensitive actions per ward: Cool roofs, drinking water kiosks, emergency cooling shelters, adjusted outdoor work shifts, greening corridors.

5. **Interactive What-If Scenario Simulator**:
   - Switchable / toggleable interventions:
     1. Cool Roofs & Reflective Surface Painting (% coverage slider)
     2. Urban Tree Canopy & Shade Cover (% increase slider)
     3. Combined intervention mode
   - Real-time recalculation showing the reduced risk score, prevented risk tier shift, and temperature reduction estimate.

6. **Live Weather Integration (Open-Meteo)**:
   - Toggle between Live Pune Current Weather (Open-Meteo API) and Simulated Peak Summer Heatwave Scenario (42°C+ peak May baseline).

## 3. Pune Ward Profiles (Prototype)
1. **Kasba Peth & Historical Core**:
   - Ultra-dense old city, high surface heat, narrow street canyons, minimal canopy, high vulnerable resident density. Risk: Very High.
2. **Hadapsar & Magarpatta Outskirts**:
   - Industrial edge & commercial sprawl, heavy vehicular corridors, elevated air pollution, concrete surfaces. Risk: High.
3. **Shivajinagar**:
   - Central civic & transit hub, high daytime heat, moderate tree buffer (COEP, Agricultural college proximity). Risk: Moderate.
4. **Kothrud**:
   - Western residential valley, established residential colonies, moderate hill breezes, medium vulnerability. Risk: Moderate.
5. **Viman Nagar / Kharadi**:
   - Modern IT corridor, glass & concrete heat retention, newer parks, lower demographic vulnerability. Risk: Moderate.
6. **Koregaon Park**:
   - High mature banyan tree canopy, lower building density, river proximity, high socioeconomic resilience. Risk: Low.

## 4. UI/UX Specifications
- **Theme**: Clean Civic Light Theme (White, slate gray, subtle borders, high-clarity typography, municipal badge accents).
- **Layout**: Top civic header with quick Pune city summary; Left 60% interactive Leaflet ward map; Right 40% ward detail dossier, factor breakdown chart, playbook, and interactive What-If simulator.
